# OPERATING_GUIDE.md — Operating Guide

## Core Directives

1. **Velocity First:** Build zero-cost, zero-maintenance client utilities that convert instantly.
2. **Customer Zero:** Chris & reseller community test real thermal printers (Dymo/Rollo) to ensure exact margins & pixel-perfect barcode reads.
3. **No Monoliths:** Keep UI, Barcode engine, and Export modules strictly separated.
