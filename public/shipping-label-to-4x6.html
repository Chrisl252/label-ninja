    01 / Four stepsHow to convert a shipping label to 4x6

    1. Download the label PDF from your marketplace or shipping service as you normally would. A letter-size or A4 file is fine.
    2. Drop the file (or several) into the converter above. Each page is checked for the label automatically.
    3. Look at the preview. If the box misses part of the label, drag its corners, or pick a different label position. Barcodes must be fully inside the box.
    4. Select Make 4 × 6 PDF, then download or open the file and print at 100% on 4 × 6 labels.

    Labels from PDF files stay as sharp as the original: the converter places the original page content on the new 4 × 6 page instead of turning it into a picture. The label is scaled to fit and centred. A landscape label on a letter page is turned to portrait automatically, or you can choose the rotation yourself.

    If a PDF is password-protected against editing, the converter copies that page as a 300 dpi image instead, which is still clear for barcodes on a 203 or 300 dpi thermal printer.

    02 / eBayeBay shipping label not 4x6?

    When eBay gives you a label on a full 8.5 × 11 page, the label usually sits in one part of the page with other information around it. Sent to a 4 × 6 thermal printer, the whole page gets shrunk, so the label prints small, or the printer cuts most of it off.

    To fix a label you already bought, drop the PDF into the converter. Auto-detect looks for the label's border; if it picks the wrong area, choose “Letter page, label in top half” and adjust the box. To get 4 × 6 labels from eBay next time, check the label format or printer setting in eBay's shipping label flow before you print. For more on this, see why an eBay label is not 4x6.

    03 / Letter-size labelsUSPS, Pirate Ship and other 8.5x11 labels to 4x6

    Many shipping services produce letter-size label PDFs for home printers. A common layout puts one label in the top half of the page with instructions or a receipt below it. Some services can also produce 4 × 6 labels directly. Pirate Ship, for example, has a label size setting. If you already downloaded the letter-size file, converting it here is usually quicker than buying the label again.

    For a page with two labels side by side or one above the other, add the file, crop the first label, then add the same file again and crop the second one. Each crop becomes its own 4 × 6 page. There is a longer walk-through in 8.5x11 shipping label to 4x6.

    04 / ReturnsConvert an Amazon return label to 4x6

    Amazon return labels are usually a letter-size PDF with the carrier label and return instructions on the same page. Choose “Amazon return label (letter)” or keep auto-detect, check that the full label and its barcodes are inside the box, then make the 4 × 6 PDF. Some returns offer a QR code for drop-off instead of a printed label; in that case there is nothing to convert. See also printing an Amazon return label on 4x6.

    05 / Printer settingsPrinting 4x6 labels on Rollo, Zebra, Munbyn and DYMO 4XL

    The converted PDF is exactly 4 × 6 in (288 × 432 points) or 100 × 150 mm per page. Most sizing problems come from the print dialog, not the file. In every case: choose the label printer, set the paper size to 4 × 6 in or 100 × 150 mm, print at 100% or Actual size, and print one page first to check.

    Where to set the label size
    PrinterWhat to check
    RolloIn the printer's preferences (Windows) or the paper size list (Mac), select 4 × 6 or 100 × 150 mm. If labels drift, run the printer's auto-calibration for your label roll.
    ZebraIn the Zebra driver's printing preferences, set the stock width 4.00 in and height 6.00 in. Darkness and speed live in the same place if barcodes look faint.
    MunbynPick the 4 × 6 or 100 × 150 mm paper size in the driver or the Munbyn app, then print at 100%.
    DYMO LabelWriter 4XL / 5XLSelect the 4 in × 6 in shipping label paper size in the print dialog. Load 4 × 6 labels; smaller DYMO labels will not fit this page size.

    If the label still prints tiny or rotated, the viewer is probably fitting the page to a different paper size. Re-open the PDF, confirm the paper size matches 4 × 6, and switch off any “fit” or “shrink” option. The label printing troubleshooting guide covers more cases.

    06 / QuestionsCommon questions

    Are my labels uploaded anywhere?

    No. The page loads the converter code once, then your browser reads, crops and builds the PDF on your own device. Label files are not sent to Label Ninja or any other server, and nothing is saved after you close the tab.

    Is it free? Are there limits?

    Yes, it is free with no account and no batch limit. The practical limit is your device's memory; very large batches may be slow on older phones.

    Will the converted label scan?

    Labels from PDF files keep their original quality because the page content is reused rather than re-drawn as a picture. Make sure the crop includes the whole barcode, print at 100%, and test-scan one label if your carrier is strict.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    Yes, in a current mobile browser. Printing from a phone depends on your printer's app supporting 4 × 6 PDFs; if the paper size cannot be set, open the downloaded file on a computer.

    Does Label Ninja buy postage or connect to eBay or Amazon?

    No. This tool only reshapes labels you already have. Label Ninja is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay, Amazon, USPS, Pirate Ship or any printer brand.
