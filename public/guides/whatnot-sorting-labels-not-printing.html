Whatnot Sorting Labels not printing: setup and fixes

If Whatnot Sorting Labels are not printing, check four things: Sorting Labels is turned on, Whatnot Tray version 2.2.6 or newer is running, the same show is open on whatnot.com on that computer, and the printer is online and connected.

Short answer

Sorting Labels print through Whatnot Tray on a computer, not from the phone app alone. Keep Tray running and keep the show open on whatnot.com on the same computer for the whole show. If labels print but come out blank, sideways, cut off or with skipped labels in between, recalibrate or restart the printer and make sure the size chosen in Sorting Labels matches the roll that is loaded.

Nothing comes outSorting Labels not printing: the four checks

These are the checks Whatnot gives for a sorting label that does not print. Go through them in order.

  1. Sorting Labels is turned on. Open the show, then More > Seller Tools > Sorting Labels, and confirm the switch is on and the sale type you are running (Auctions, Buy It Now, Giveaways, Breaks/Surprise Sets) is selected.
  2. Whatnot Tray is running, version 2.2.6 or newer. Tray is Whatnot's free desktop app that sends each label to your printer. It has to stay open in the background.
  3. The same show is open on whatnot.com on that computer. Tray alone is not enough. Keep both open for the whole show.
  4. The printer is online and connected to the computer. If the printer does not appear in Whatnot Tray, Whatnot says to make sure it is installed and shows up in your computer's printer settings.

Also check your word filters. Sorting Labels has optional word filters that control which items generate labels, and a label only prints when a sale matches your settings.

Source: Whatnot: Use Sorting Labels to organize sold items.

Before the showWhat Sorting Labels need

  • A computer you can keep running during your show.
  • Whatnot Tray version 2.2.6 or newer, installed and left running in the background.
  • A label printer that is installed and set up on that computer.
  • Label stock in one of the supported sizes (roll width by label height): 1 × 1 in, 2 × 1 in, 3 × 2 in or 4 × 6 in.

Not sure which roll to buy? Whatnot label sizes: which stock for each label type compares the Sorting Labels sizes with shipping and number label sizes.

One-time setupWhere Sorting Labels are in Whatnot

Whatnot says you only set this up once on whatnot.com. After that, labels print for future shows using your saved preferences.

  1. From the Shows page on whatnot.com, open your upcoming or active show.
  2. Select More > Seller Tools > Sorting Labels.
  3. Turn on Sorting Labels.
  4. Choose which sale types generate labels: Auctions, Buy It Now (BINs), Giveaways, Breaks/Surprise Sets.
  5. If needed, download, install and open Whatnot Tray.
  6. In the Sorting Labels panel, select Connect to Whatnot Tray. If Tray asks for permission, select Allow. Selecting "Remember this decision" stops it asking again.
  7. Select your printer and label size.
  8. Configure what prints on the label, such as item name and show name.
  9. Optionally, add word filters to control which items generate labels.

Then print a test label from the same panel. It shows how a live sorting label will look before any sale depends on it.

It prints, but wrongBlank, sideways, cut-off or skipped labels

Whatnot's first fix is to recalibrate and/or restart the printer. The calibration steps depend on the printer, so look them up for your exact model. Whatnot also recommends recalibrating or restarting any time you change the label size in Sorting Labels or swap the roll.

If that does not fix it, Whatnot says to check two things:

  • The loaded stock matches the selected size. The physical label size in the printer has to match the label size chosen in your Sorting Labels settings.
  • The media edge guides fit the roll. The guides keep labels centered and straight as they feed. How you adjust them varies by printer.

If you have tried all of that and it still fails, Whatnot's page says to contact Whatnot support. Label Ninja is independent and cannot see or change your Whatnot settings.

Odd-size rollsYour label size is not in the list

Sizes in Sorting Labels are listed as width × height: the first number is the width of the roll and the second is the height of each label. Choose the closest available size that is the same as or smaller than your labels in both dimensions.

Whatnot's example: with 2.25 × 1.25 inch labels, choose 2 × 1 inch. There will be a little blank space, and the label still prints normally. Do not choose a larger size. If the print area is larger than the label, the printer may feed blank labels or split the content across two labels.

Less obvious causesPhone hosting, breaks and Tray versions

You host from your phone

That works. You can use the phone as your camera or main device, but Whatnot Tray still has to be running and the show has to be open on whatnot.com on your computer for labels to print.

Breaks and Surprise Sets

A label does not print when someone buys a spot. It prints when the buyer is assigned their item. Labels only print while you are live, so randomize before you end the show. If you assign items after the show ends, those labels will not print.

Which Whatnot Tray download

Tray comes in arm64 and x86_64 versions. On a Mac, open the Apple menu > About This Mac: an Apple M-series chip needs arm64 and an Intel chip needs x86_64. On Windows, most computers use x86_64. To check, go to Settings > System > About and read System type. If it says ARM-based processor, download arm64.

Sorting Labels are not shipping labels

They are for organizing sold items. Postage is separate: generate shipping labels from the Shipments page as usual. If those come out tiny on a 4 × 6 printer, see how to change Whatnot label size to 4×6.

No computer at the showA manual alternative: number labels

If you cannot keep a computer and Whatnot Tray running during shows, numbered item labels are a manual way to stay organized. Print a run such as #1 to #80 before the show, stick one on each item and call the number on stream, then match numbers to buyers from your own sales list.

The free Whatnot number label maker makes those PDFs in 1 × 0.5, 2 × 1 or 3 × 2 inches. It does not connect to Whatnot and does not know who bought what, so it is not a replacement for Sorting Labels. Sorting Labels vs number labels compares the two.

Quick answersCommon questions

Why are my Whatnot Sorting Labels not printing?

Whatnot lists four things to check: Sorting Labels is turned on, Whatnot Tray version 2.2.6 or newer is running, the same show is open on whatnot.com on that computer, and the printer is online and connected to the computer.

What do I need to use Whatnot Sorting Labels?

A computer you can keep running during the show, Whatnot Tray version 2.2.6 or newer, a label printer installed on that computer, and label stock in 1 × 1, 2 × 1, 3 × 2 or 4 × 6 inches.

Can I host from my phone and still print Sorting Labels?

Yes. Whatnot says you can host the show from your phone as long as Whatnot Tray is running and the show is open on whatnot.com on your computer.

Are Sorting Labels the same as shipping labels?

No. Whatnot describes them as organizational tools. You still generate and print shipping labels from the Shipments page in Seller Hub.

Why did no label print for my break or Surprise Set?

For Breaks and Surprise Sets a label prints when the buyer is assigned their item, not when they buy a spot. Labels only print while you are live, so randomize before you end the show.
