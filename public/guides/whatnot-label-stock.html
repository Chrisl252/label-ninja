Whatnot label sizes: which stock for each label type

Whatnot shipping labels print on 4 × 6 inch thermal labels or 8.5 × 11 paper. Whatnot Sorting Labels support four stock sizes: 1 × 1, 2 × 1, 3 × 2 and 4 × 6 inches. Numbered item labels are your own system, so they can be any size your printer takes.

Short answer

Buy 4 × 6 direct thermal labels for shipping. For Sorting Labels, buy one of Whatnot's four supported sizes. If you also print number labels with Label Ninja, a 2 × 1 or 3 × 2 inch roll covers both jobs, because those two sizes are on both lists. Check your printer's minimum label width before you order anything narrower than 2 inches.

PostageShipping label stock: 4 × 6 or plain paper

Whatnot offers shipping labels in 8.5 × 11 inches (21.6 × 27.9 cm) for standard printers and 4 × 6 inches (10.2 × 15.2 cm) for thermal printers. So you do not need a thermal printer to ship: a regular printer and plain paper work with the 8.5 × 11 format.

If you use a thermal printer, load 4 × 6 labels and switch Whatnot's label size to match. The default is 8.5 × 11, and a letter-size file sent to a 4 × 6 roll prints tiny. The setting is on the Shipments page, in the Sidebar, under Default Label Options. How to change Whatnot label size to 4×6 walks through it, including labels you already generated.

Packing slips have their own format setting in the same place, with the same two sizes.

Whatnot's own featureSorting Labels stock: four supported sizes

Sorting Labels print automatically as items sell during a show. Whatnot lists these supported sizes, written as roll width by label height:

  • 1 × 1 in, or 25 × 25 mm
  • 2 × 1 in, or 50 × 25 mm
  • 3 × 2 in, or 75 × 50 mm
  • 4 × 6 in, or 100 × 150 mm

If your roll is not one of the four

Whatnot's rule is to choose the closest listed size that is the same as or smaller than your labels in both dimensions. Its example: with 2.25 × 1.25 inch labels, choose 2 × 1. You get a little blank space and the label prints normally.

Do not choose a larger size. Whatnot warns that if the print area is larger than the label, the printer may feed blank labels or split the content across two labels.

After you change rolls

Whatnot recommends recalibrating or restarting the printer whenever you change the label size in your Sorting Labels settings or swap the physical labels in the printer. If labels still come out blank, sideways or cut off, see Whatnot Sorting Labels not printing.

Your own systemNumber label stock: 1 × 0.5, 2 × 1 or 3 × 2

Number labels, also called number tags or number stickers, are not a Whatnot feature. You print a run such as #1 to #80 before the show and stick one on each item. Whatnot does not set a size for them.

The free Whatnot number label maker produces PDFs in three exact sizes:

  • 1 × 0.5 in: for small items or cards, where the number should stay out of the way.
  • 2 × 1 in: a middle size for bags and sleeves, and the default in the tool.
  • 3 × 2 in: the largest number, which is easier to read on camera.

Each PDF page is the exact stock size, so print at 100% with the printer's paper size set to the same dimensions.

One roll for Sorting Labels and number labels

2 × 1 and 3 × 2 inches appear on both lists. If you want to keep one small roll loaded for both jobs, pick one of those. 1 × 1 is on Whatnot's list only, and 1 × 0.5 is in the number label maker only.

Before you orderCheck the printer before you buy stock

  • Minimum width. A 4 × 6 printer does not always take narrow rolls. For example, the Rollo models in our 4x6 printer comparison list a label width range of 1.57 to 4.1 inches, which covers 2 inch and 3 inch wide rolls but not 1 inch wide ones. Look up the range for your exact model.
  • Direct thermal or ribbon. Rollo prints direct thermal labels only; thermal transfer stock needs a ribbon and will come out blank. Rollo's label requirements explain this. Other brands publish their own rules.
  • Measured size, not just the name. A roll sold as 2 × 1 inches is 50.8 × 25.4 mm, while a driver entry may say 51 × 25 mm. Use the measurements on the packaging when you set the printer's paper size.

Quick answersCommon questions

What size are Whatnot shipping labels?

Whatnot makes shipping labels in two sizes: 4 × 6 inches for thermal printers and 8.5 × 11 inches for standard printers. 8.5 × 11 is the default until you change it. How to change Whatnot label size to 4×6 has the menu path.

What label sizes work with Whatnot Sorting Labels?

Whatnot lists four supported stock sizes, written as roll width by label height: 1 × 1 inch (25 × 25 mm), 2 × 1 inch (50 × 25 mm), 3 × 2 inch (75 × 50 mm) and 4 × 6 inch (100 × 150 mm).

My label size is not in the Sorting Labels list. Which one do I pick?

Whatnot says to choose the closest size that is the same as or smaller than your labels in both dimensions. Its own example: for 2.25 × 1.25 inch labels, choose 2 × 1. Do not choose a larger size, or the printer may feed blank labels or split the content across two labels.

What size are number stickers for a Whatnot show?

There is no official size, because item numbers are your own system and not a Whatnot feature. The Label Ninja number label maker prints 1 × 0.5, 2 × 1 and 3 × 2 inch PDFs. 2 × 1 is the default.

Can one roll do both Sorting Labels and number labels?

Yes, if it is 2 × 1 or 3 × 2 inches. Those two sizes are on Whatnot's Sorting Labels list and in the Label Ninja number label maker.
