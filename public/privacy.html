Privacy policy

Label Ninja is a browser-based thermal label studio for warehouse bins, Whatnot show numbers, Amazon FNSKU labels, and custom barcode labels. This page says what we store, what stays on your computer, and who else touches the data.

Last updated 6 September 2026

What stays in your browser

Designing a label happens in this browser. Uploaded artwork (PNG, JPEG, or WebP up to 8 MB each), dragged elements, barcode previews, and every setting stay on your machine until you choose Download PDF or Save project. Closing the tab or clearing site data removes unsaved local work.

Accounts

Exporting a PDF requires a free account. We store your email address and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password — never the password itself. Signing in sets one cookie, ln_session, which is HttpOnly, Secure, and expires after 30 days or when you sign out. Password reset sends a single-use link to your email that expires after one hour; using it signs out every other session.

We keep a count of how many PDF exports your account has used. The free plan includes 10 PDF batches total. Each successfully generated PDF uses one batch, regardless of its label count.

PDF exports

When you click Download PDF, the label specification — text, barcode values, sizes, and any images you placed — is sent to our server, rendered into a PDF, and returned to you. The specification itself is not kept after rendering. The finished PDF remains available from My Exports for up to 7 days; expired files are removed by scheduled cleanup, which normally runs every 15 minutes, and during later export activity. Cleanup may be delayed by service outages or a backlog. You can delete an export sooner from the same drawer.

Saved projects

If you save a project, its name and settings — including any label text and artwork it contains — are stored with your account until you delete the project. Nothing is saved unless you choose Save.

Billing

Pro subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout and billing pages and never touch Label Ninja's servers. We store your Stripe customer id, subscription id, subscription status, and paid-through date so the site knows what your account is entitled to.

Abuse protection and logs

Sign-up, sign-in, and password-reset requests are rate-limited by IP address (10 per hour). Export requests are rate-limited per account (30 per hour). Server logs record request outcomes and error codes, not the content of your labels.

Advertising

Label Ninja's tools are ad-free. If advertising returns, it will appear only on public guide pages, in clearly labeled slots, and those ad companies may use cookies or similar identifiers to choose and measure ads inside their frames. We do not run pop-unders, social bars, or smartlink redirects.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare hosts the site, the API, and the database, and sees requests the way any host does.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this page.
  • jsDelivr serves the barcode-preview and CSV-parsing libraries the tools load.
  • Stripe handles payment, as described above.
  • Resend delivers password-reset emails when email recovery is configured. It receives your email address and the recovery link.

Your browser's cookie and tracking settings control what those services can store.

Children

Label Ninja is built for sellers and warehouse work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.

Contact
