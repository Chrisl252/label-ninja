Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Label Ninja is a free browser tool for thermal labels, barcodes, warehouse bins, Whatnot numbers, and Amazon FNSKU labels. It is operated by Bisket LLC. This page says what we collect, what stays on your computer, and how advertising works.

What stays in your browser

Label artwork, uploaded images, CSV manifests, and print output are processed in this browser. We do not create accounts, and we do not upload your files to a Label Ninja server. Closing the tab or clearing site data removes that local work. Print jobs go to the printer you choose on your own machine.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or password signup.
  • No payment form on this site.
  • No server-side store of label images or CSV files.

Advertising

Label Ninja's tools are currently ad-free. If we add advertising to public guide pages in the future, it will run in clearly labeled slots, and those ad companies may use cookies or similar identifiers to choose and measure ads. That would happen inside the ad frames, not inside your label files.

We do not run pop-unders, social bars, or smartlink redirects on Label Ninja.

Cookies and third-party scripts

The tools load libraries from public CDNs so barcodes, CSV parsing, PDF output, and OCR can run in the browser. Your browser's cookie and tracking settings control how much of that is stored.

Children

Label Ninja is built for sellers and warehouse work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.

Contact

Label Ninja is operated by Bisket LLC. Privacy questions can be opened as an issue on the Label Ninja GitHub repository.
